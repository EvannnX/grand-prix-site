Credits

Game design, prompts, 3D cleanup and part labelling: Bang Xiao, for the AI Writathon in Perspectives on the Humanities, NYU Shanghai, Fall 2026.

Drivers: cartoon caricatures generated with Image 2 and turned into rigged 3D models with Tripo AI. They are shown without names, as agreed with the course instructor.

Karts: generated with Image 2 and Tripo AI, split into parts with Tripo Smart Segment.

Tracks (CC BY 4.0, by amogusstrikesback2 on Sketchfab, track names in this game are new): Map_njxz (Flower Loop), Map_tkgcz (Sky Plaza Climb), Map_qms (Mountain Run). License: creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0. Models were rescaled and compressed; racing lines were computed from the road meshes.

Engine: three.js (MIT). Code: written with Claude.
